# Competitor research register

**Reviewed:** 27 September 2026. **Method:** current public official vendor pages. No vendor account was purchased or operated for this review. No hands-on timing, usability, accuracy or assistant benchmark was conducted.

The production comparison tables link the relevant sources beside each vendor claim in both the desktop table and mobile cards. Each page has a visible reviewed date, source list and scope statement. The pages are published by QuoteCore+, not presented as an independent study.

Public documentation establishes advertised capability, not measured performance. A capability not established by these sources is described as unverified, not absent. A roadmap or preview is not treated as general availability. Product availability, taxes, billing terms, volumes, seat counts and regional offers can change; recheck the linked page before publishing a materially later release.

QuoteCore+ product claims are grounded separately in `RETURN_NOTES_PHASE2.md` and the V3 handoff. They are not inferred from competitor documentation. No new claim is made that Assistant performs geometry, creates orders/invoices or operates autonomously.

## Important prior-claim decisions

Every original comparison row topic is retained. Categorical unsupported feature claims are not repeated as if newly verified. In particular, the prior Bluebeam slope-aware row was positive, while the reviewed public sources did not establish the precise automatic roof-pitch workflow. It remains a row, now qualified as unverified, and is explicitly flagged for the owner in `OWNER_DECISIONS_COMPETITOR.md`. This qualification is not a finding that Bluebeam lacks slope or formula capability.

The same distinction applies to HOVER direct geometry editing and Roofr reviewed AI plan detection. No absence claim or competitive score is inferred from a failed documentation lookup. No universal easiest/best/most accurate/only-assistant claim was added.
