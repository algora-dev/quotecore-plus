## Architecture fact you must understand first

QuoteCore+ is ONE Next.js application, ONE build, ONE deployment. The public marketing site and the product app are split by HOSTNAME in middleware:

- `quote-core.com` → marketing pages + free tools (your surface)
- `app.quote-core.com` → the product app (out of scope)

That is why you receive the full repository: the marketing site cannot build standalone, and your return must compile against the whole tree. Your brief below locks your WRITABLE SURFACE to marketing only. Everything else is read-only context.

## Why this mission exists — the product truth (2026-09)

The product has gained major capability the marketing site does not yet reflect:

1. **Full mobile takeoff** — the entire measure→quote flow now runs on phones/tablets: AI roof-plan scan on device, component scan, visual component picking, save & continue. Mobile is now a first-class customer base, not a compromise.
2. **Smart Assistant V2** — in-app AI chat that answers workspace questions and performs propose-then-confirm actions (e.g. adjusting rates) across quotes, orders and invoices.
3. **Document Studio** — redesigned quote/order/invoice editors with shared professional renderers for in-app, public links and PDF export.
4. **Staged AI scan on desktop** — scan → correct vertices on plan → auto component detection.

Marketing must mirror this story — especially the mobile capability, which opens a customer base the current site doesn't speak to.

## Hard product facts for all copy (audit every page against these)

1. **PAID-ONLY app. No free trial. No free plan. No free signup.** Flow: signup → onboarding → paywall → pay → workspace. Plans: **Starter / Pro / Pro Plus** plus **Done-For-You setup**.
2. A **30-day money-back guarantee** replaces the free trial.
3. **PRICES ARE OWNER-LOCKED.** Do not change any price numbers anywhere. Presentation of pricing may improve; values may not. (A repricing is planned separately by the owner.)
4. Real status vocabulary for any product screenshots/descriptions: quotes `draft|confirmed|sent|accepted|declined|expired|archived`; invoices `draft|sent|viewed|payment_reported|paid|disputed|cancelled`; orders `ready|ordered`.
