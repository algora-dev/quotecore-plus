# Marketing Phase 3 — Blog + Free Tools migration

## Acquisition rule
Public content may teach, compare and link to relevant free tools, but commercial conversion should resolve to only two paths:

1. **Book 25 minutes with Shaun** — tell Shaun how you work, see where QuoteCore+ could help, and receive his #1 tip for winning more quoted jobs.
2. **Create a Free Workspace** — real account, no card, no expiry, optional Guided Walkthrough.

The Guided Walkthrough remains a feature inside the Free Workspace, not the primary public acquisition product. Exact plan configuration/pricing belongs inside authenticated Billing.

## Free tools rule
Standalone free tools remain genuinely useful and may link to other free tools where that is directly useful to the task. They must not describe the QuoteCore+ app as paid-only, require a plan before access, or push a public demo/pricing builder as the next step. "Save to QuoteCore+" should provision/open a Free Workspace when necessary.

## Blog rule
Educational links to free tools remain when editorially relevant. End-of-article commercial conversion uses the shared two-path CTA. Old claims about interactive demos, paid configurations before access, pricing builders, and 20-minute calls must be removed.

## Phase 3 freeze notes
- Blog end-of-article conversion now uses the shared two-path CTA.
- Legacy article-body product claims were updated while preserving factual competitor trials and genuine free-tool references.
- Free-tool account messaging is explicitly separate from the QuoteCore+ Free Workspace.
- Shared save-to-app/document/calculator bridges now create/open a Free Workspace rather than requiring a paid plan.
- The Free Tools hub/header keeps tool-account controls as utilities while commercial CTAs prioritize Shaun and Free Workspace.
- The site assistant now explains the current Free Workspace, Guided Walkthrough and authenticated Billing model.
- Cross-codebase sanity fixes also removed stale 20-minute/demo-first language from feature-request email, legacy billing notice and demo conversion overlays.
- Intentional `/demo` links remain only where they launch, reset or navigate within the isolated Guided Walkthrough/demo infrastructure.
