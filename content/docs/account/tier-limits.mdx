# Usage & storage caps

Every plan has caps. With QuoteCore+ you choose your own: the usage allowance and storage on your workspace come from what you configured in the [pricing calculator](https://quote-core.com/pricing) when you subscribed. This page explains what each cap means in practice and what happens when you reach one.

## The five caps

| Cap | What it counts | Reset cycle |
|---|---|---|
| **Quotes / month** | Every quote you create (draft or sent) | 1st of each month, UTC |
| **Components (lifetime)** | Active components in your library | Never - manual delete only |
| **Drawings & Images (lifetime)** | Drawing and image designs in your library | Never - manual delete only |
| **Material orders / month** | Outgoing orders sent to suppliers | 1st of each month, UTC |
| **Storage** | Total bytes uploaded across the company | Never - manual delete only |

Your specific numbers are shown in **Account → Billing**. Features you didn't include in your plan are locked rather than capped - the page shows an upgrade prompt instead of the feature.

## Quotes per month

The most important cap. Both **draft quotes** and **finalised quotes** count toward this limit. A new quote consumes one slot when it's created.

<Callout type="warning">
Sending or finalising a draft does **not** free up its slot. The counter is "quotes created", not "quotes pending". This is intentional - it prevents people from cycling drafts to dodge the cap.
</Callout>

When you hit the monthly cap:

- The **+ New Quote** button on the quotes list page greys out.
- Clicking it opens an upgrade prompt: *"Monthly quote limit reached (X/Y). To create more quotes this month you need to adjust your plan, or wait until your quote limit resets."*
- The cap resets at midnight UTC on the 1st of next month.

## Components (lifetime)

Components are the building blocks of every quote (ridge cap, valley flashing, fascia, etc.). The lifetime cap counts every **active** component in your library.

- Soft-deleted components (set inactive) **do not count**. Hard-deleting is the only way to make room if you've toggled instead of deleted.
- New accounts are seeded with 8 starter components, so you start at 8 of your limit.
- When at the cap, the **+ Add Component** button on the components page opens an upgrade prompt.

## Drawings & Images (lifetime)

Same model as components, but for the drawings & images library. These are images + drawings of cut metal pieces, technical drawings, or reference images that go onto material orders.

- Every drawing or uploaded image counts as 1.
- Editing an existing drawing or image does not consume a new slot.
- When at the cap, both **Create** and **Upload** buttons on the drawings & images page open an upgrade prompt.
- If drawings & images isn't part of your plan, the whole page shows an upgrade splash and the "Drawings & Images" entry button on the components page opens the prompt instead of navigating.

## Material orders per month

Outgoing purchase orders sent to suppliers. Resets monthly like quotes. If material orders isn't part of your plan, the nav entry opens an upgrade prompt instead of routing through.

## Storage

Total bytes uploaded to your company storage. Counts:

- Roof plan PDFs and images uploaded for digital takeoff
- Photos attached to quotes
- Company logo
- Drawing and image reference files

Does **not** count:

- Generated PDFs (created on the fly when you click download)
- Customer-facing accept page renders

When you're near the limit:

- A storage usage bar on the billing page shows your usage.
- At 90% it turns amber.
- At 100% the next upload fails with a "Storage quota exceeded" error.

To free up space, delete old quote attachments or roof plans you no longer need. Need more headroom permanently? Adjust your storage in the [pricing calculator](https://quote-core.com/pricing) and re-subscribe.

## How the upgrade prompt works

Every cap opens the same pattern:

- Title naming the specific cap you hit
- Description explaining what's blocked
- **Not now** button to dismiss
- **View plans** button that takes you to **Account → Billing**

## What if I reduce my plan?

If you adjust your plan down and end up with **more existing data than the new caps allow**, that's fine:

- We never delete data when a plan changes.
- Caps only block *new* additions.
- Example: a customer with 30 components who reduces to a 15-component allowance keeps all 30 components. They just can't add a new one until there's room.

## Where to see your current usage

- **Billing page** - storage bar and usage counters.
- **Quotes page** - quotes-this-month counter (top right of the list).
- **Components page** - `X/Y` counter inside the + Add Component button.
- **Drawings & Images page** - `X/Y used` next to the Create/Upload buttons.
