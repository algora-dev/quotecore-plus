# Plans & billing

QuoteCore+ is a paid product. There is **no free trial** - instead, every paid plan is covered by a **30-day money-back guarantee**, so you can try the full product risk-free.

The free [trade calculators and tools](/docs/help/free-tools) are available to everyone with no signup, if you want to get a feel for the platform before paying.

## The three plans

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $19/mo | Solo contractors getting started with manual quoting |
| **Professional** | $39/mo | QuoteCore+ power users - digital takeoff, drawings & images, follow-ups, catalogs, message center |
| **Pro Plus** | $59/mo | Teams - higher caps and 3 included seats |

All three plans include:

- Manual quotes, digital takeoff, blank quotes (Professional and above)
- Customer-facing quote pages with accept / change / decline flow
- Email send from inside the app
- Drawing & image designs and library (Professional and above)
- Material orders to your suppliers
- Automated follow-ups
- Activity card on every quote

For the full caps breakdown (quotes per month, components, drawings & images, material orders, storage), see [Tier limits](/docs/account/tier-limits).

## The 30-day money-back guarantee

If you subscribe to any paid plan and it isn't right for you, you can request a full refund within **30 days of your initial payment**. No questions asked.

<Steps>

1. [Contact support](/docs/help/support-and-contact) within 30 days of your first payment.
2. We process the refund and cancel the subscription.
3. Your data stays accessible - nothing is deleted on refund.

</Steps>

The guarantee replaces a free trial: rather than capping what you can do while you evaluate, you get the full product from day one and a 30-day window to get your money back.

## Picking a plan

<Steps>

1. Sign up for an account.
2. Go to **Account > Billing**.
3. Compare plans in the grid - click **View** on any plan for the full feature breakdown.
4. Click **Choose &lt;plan&gt;** on the plan you want and complete the Stripe checkout.

</Steps>

Your account activates within a few seconds of payment being confirmed, and the 30-day money-back window starts from your first payment.

## Upgrading, downgrading, and cancelling

- **Upgrade or downgrade** - use **Manage subscription** on the billing page to open the Stripe customer portal. Stripe applies the prorated difference immediately.
- **Cancel** - click **Cancel subscription** in the portal. You keep paid features until the end of your current billing period, and your data is never deleted.
- **Reactivate** - pick any paid plan from **Account > Billing** at any time.

For the step-by-step walkthrough, see [Upgrading and cancelling](/docs/account/upgrading-and-cancelling).

## Frequently asked

**Is there a free trial?**
No. QuoteCore+ doesn't offer a free trial. Every paid plan comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee instead, so you can try the full product risk-free.

**Will I be charged during the first 30 days?**
Yes - you pay when you subscribe. If you request a refund within 30 days, you get your money back in full.

**Can I switch plans mid-period?**
Yes. Switches happen through the Stripe customer portal and are prorated automatically.

**Can I export my data if I don't want to continue?**
Yes - your quotes and customer data stay accessible. Each quote has a download option, and material orders can be exported from the orders page. For a full data export contact support.
