# Plans & billing

QuoteCore+ doesn't come in fixed tiers. **Plans start from $29 US/month**, and you configure the rest yourself: pick the features you need, the amount of use you expect, and how much storage you want. The price follows your choices - nothing more.

## Build your plan

The [pricing calculator](https://quote-core.com/pricing) shows exactly how each choice changes the monthly price before you commit to anything. Adjust the features, usage and storage and watch the price update - no surprises at checkout.

Rather be walked through it? The same page lets you book a free demo call and we'll configure the plan with you.

## Paying

1. Build your plan on the [pricing page](https://quote-core.com/pricing).
2. Checkout is handled by **Stripe** - cards, receipts and invoicing are all processed there.
3. Your configuration and usage are shown in **Account → Billing**, with counters for the current billing period.

## What you'll see in Account → Billing

- **Your plan** - the features, usage allowance and storage you selected.
- **Usage counters** - how much of each allowance you've used this billing period, with a progress bar.
- **Manage subscription** - update your card, download receipts, or adjust your plan.

Want different caps or features mid-period? See [Upgrading and cancelling](/docs/account/upgrading-and-cancelling). Not a customer yet? Try the [guided demo](/docs/getting-started/demo) first - no signup needed.
