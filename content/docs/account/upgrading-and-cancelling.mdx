# Changing or cancelling your plan

Plan changes happen in two places: the [pricing calculator](https://quote-core.com/pricing) for your configuration, and **Account > Billing → Manage subscription** (Stripe's portal) for payment matters. Stripe handles payment, card storage and invoicing - you never enter card details into QuoteCore+ itself.

## Subscribing (new account)

<Steps>

1. Build the plan you want on the [pricing page](https://quote-core.com/pricing) - choose your features, expected usage and storage. The calculator shows the monthly price as you go.
2. Checkout opens on `checkout.stripe.com` with your configured price.
3. Enter card details and confirm. Stripe charges you immediately.
4. You're redirected back to **Account > Billing** with your plan active and your usage counters ready.

</Steps>

## Adjusting your plan

To change your configuration (features, usage allowance, storage):

1. Reconfigure on the [pricing page](https://quote-core.com/pricing) and checkout with the new setup, or
2. Open **Account > Billing → Manage subscription** to update your plan in Stripe's customer portal - it shows any proration (a partial charge or a credit on your next invoice) before you confirm.

Either way you end up with one subscription at your new configuration - no double billing.

## Cancelling

<Steps>

1. Open **Account > Billing**.
2. Click **Manage subscription** on the current-plan card.
3. In the portal, click **Cancel subscription**.
4. Confirm. You're brought back to QuoteCore+.

</Steps>

After you cancel:

- You **keep paid features** until the end of the current billing period - you've paid for it, you get to use it.
- At period end, the subscription closes and your workspace loses its paid features.
- Your data is **never deleted** on cancellation.

## Reactivating a cancelled subscription

- **Before period end** (winding down): use **Manage subscription** in the portal to resume.
- **After period end**: subscribe again from the [pricing page](https://quote-core.com/pricing).

## Updating your card

1. Click **Manage subscription** on the billing page.
2. Click **Update payment method**.
3. Add the new card. The old card stays on file until you delete it.

Cards are never stored in QuoteCore+. They live on Stripe's PCI-compliant infrastructure.

## Downloading invoices

1. Click **Manage subscription** on the billing page.
2. Scroll to **Billing history**.
3. Click any invoice to download it as a PDF.

Invoices are also emailed to the address on your Stripe customer record after each successful payment.

## What if my payment fails?

Stripe automatically retries failed cards on a schedule (typically day 0, 3, 5, 7 after failure). During this time:

- Your **status** moves to `past_due` then `grace`.
- You keep **read access** to everything.
- New mutations (creating quotes, sending orders) are blocked in `grace`.
- A banner on the billing page tells you what to do.

Update your card in the portal as soon as you can. The moment a payment succeeds, your status returns to `active`. See [Billing](/docs/account/billing) for the full timeline.

## Disputes (chargebacks)

If you dispute a charge with your bank:

- We're notified automatically. A **support ticket** is opened on your account.
- Your subscription status becomes `disputed` while the bank reviews.
- When the dispute closes, the ticket auto-resolves and your status returns to normal (or, if the chargeback succeeded, your subscription is cancelled).

For anything else, [contact support](/docs/help/support-and-contact) first - we'd rather fix it than fight it.
