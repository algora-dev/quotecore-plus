# Mobile & installing the app

Every part of QuoteCore+ works on your phone — takeoff, quotes, customer approvals, material orders, invoices. The layouts are built for touch and small screens, not squeezed-down desktop pages.

## Install it as an app

QuoteCore+ is a progressive web app (PWA). Installing gives you a full-screen experience with no browser chrome in the way — it behaves like a native app:

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the menu (⋮) → **Install app**.

Sign in once and you stay signed in, like any app.

## Why install?

- **Full screen.** The whole display is your workspace — no address bar eating space.
- **Home-screen icon.** Launches straight into the app like any other app on your phone.
- **Same account, same data.** Phone and desktop stay in sync — start a quote at the office, finish it on the roof.
