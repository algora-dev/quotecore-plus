# Smart Component Creator

The Smart Component Creator is a free tool that lets you pick a material type, enter your measurements, and get a complete material list with waste applied. It works the same way as the Smart Component tab inside the trade calculators, but as a standalone page.

## Access

- **URL:** [/free-smart-component-creator](/free-smart-component-creator)
- **Cost:** Free, no signup required.
- **Units:** Metric and imperial supported.

## How it works

1. **Pick a material type** - e.g. roofing iron, tiles, underlay, fixings.
2. **Enter your measurements** - area, length, or quantity depending on the material.
3. **Set waste percentage** - the system applies waste to give you realistic quantities.
4. **Review the material list** - the system calculates quantities, pack sizes, and total costs (if you enter a unit price).
5. **Download or print** - get a PDF of the material list.

## Saving to QuoteCore+

If you have a QuoteCore+ account:

- **Smart Component drafts** are saved server-side automatically.
- When you sign in to the app, your dashboard shows a "Restore draft" banner.
- Click **Add to Component Library** to convert the Smart Component spec into a full component in your QuoteCore+ library - waste routing, pack pricing, pitch type, and component slot requirements are mapped automatically.
