# Smart Assistant

The Smart Assistant is the advanced AI assistant built into the QuoteCore+ app. Unlike Q — the free assistant that helps you find your way around the app — the Smart Assistant works on your actual workspace: it finds, reads and summarises your quotes, drafts, invoices and orders, and can carry structured work with you step by step.

It's part of the paid app. Q answers questions about QuoteCore+; the Smart Assistant answers questions about *your* business.

## What it can do

- **Find anything, fast.** "Find the quote for 12 Acacia Avenue", "latest invoice for Smith", "how many quotes did we send this month?" — it searches your real records and gives you a button to open the right page.
- **Read the numbers.** Ask for a quote's total, its components or its roof areas and it reads the authoritative values straight from your workspace — not guesses.
- **Your day at a glance.** Ask what needs attention: customers who viewed a quote but haven't accepted, suppliers who haven't replied, overdue invoices and follow-ups due today.
- **Structured work.** It can start multi-step jobs — like a new draft — and continue them with you by text or voice, keeping every step on the record.
- **Voice.** Talk instead of type where your browser supports it.

## It suggests, you decide

The Smart Assistant only sees records you're authorised to see. Anything that changes or deletes your data asks for your confirmation first — navigation and suggestions never edit anything on their own.

## Q or Smart Assistant?

Two assistants, two jobs. If you're not sure which one you're talking to, see [Q vs the Smart Assistant](/docs/smart-assistant/q-vs-smart-assistant). Short version: Q is the free guide; this is the paid one that does real work on your data.
