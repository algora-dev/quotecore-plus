# Supplier dashboard

If your company is set up as a **supplier** (by the QuoteCore+ admin team), you get access to the supplier dashboard. This is where you publish component libraries that other QuoteCore+ users can browse and import from.

## Getting supplier access

Supplier accounts are created manually by the QuoteCore+ admin team. If you want your company set up as a supplier:

1. Contact support from **Account > Support**.
2. The admin team verifies your business and creates a supplier profile.
3. Your company dashboard gets a **Supplier** tab.

Only approved suppliers can publish libraries. All users can browse and import.

## Managing your supplier profile

In the **Supplier** tab:

- **Supplier name** - your trading name (must be unique across QuoteCore+).
- **Description** - tell users what you supply.
- **Website URL** - link to your website.
- **Service areas** - regions you deliver to.
- **Roofing types** - e.g. long-run, tile, membrane.
- **Product categories** - e.g. roofing iron, underlay, flashings, fixings.
- **Brands** - brands you stock.
- **Keywords** - help users find you in the directory.

Your profile must be **approved** by the admin team before it appears in the public directory.

## Publishing a component library

1. Go to your **Components** page.
2. Create components as normal, filling in SKU, price, product type, and notes.
3. Components must have a **SKU** to be published (this is how we track updates).
4. Click **Publish Library** to open the publication modal.
5. Set:
   - **Visibility** - Published (visible in directory), Unlisted (link only), or Private.
   - **Public title and description** - what directory browsers see.
   - **Roofing types, product categories, brands, keywords** - for search filtering.
6. Click **Publish**. Your library is now live in the directory.

### Publishing an update

When you change prices, add components, or modify existing ones:

1. Click **Publish Update** on the components page.
2. The system detects what changed since the last published version (added, modified, removed, price changed).
3. Users who imported your components get a pending update notification.
4. They choose whether to accept or dismiss each change.

Version numbers increment automatically with each published update.

## Catalogue converter

The catalogue converter lets suppliers turn a CSV price list into QuoteCore+ components quickly. Instead of manually creating each component, you upload a CSV and map columns.

### Using the catalogue converter

1. Go to your **Supplier** tab and click **Catalogue Converter**.
2. **Upload a CSV file** - up to 30,000+ rows supported.
3. **Map columns** - tell the system which columns map to:
   - SKU (required)
   - Component name (required)
   - Price (required)
   - Product type
   - Notes
4. **Review** - see a preview of how the rows will become components.
5. **Convert** - the system creates components in your library from the CSV rows.

Only 5 fields are mapped (SKU, name, price, product type, notes) to keep the process fast. You can edit the full component details (waste, pitch, measurement type, etc.) after conversion.

### Column mapping

The converter supports:

- **Multi-map** - create multiple column maps over the same CSV (e.g. trade price vs retail price).
- **Name limit** - component names are capped at 60 characters.
- **SKU immutability** - once a component has a SKU, the SKU cannot be changed (so update tracking works correctly).
- **Column highlighting** - selected and mapped columns are visually highlighted during mapping.
- **Infinite scroll** - large catalogues load progressively.
